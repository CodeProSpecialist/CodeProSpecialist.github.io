Privacy policy

Fennarith Algebra Solver Pro · last updated 17 November 2026

What stays on your phone

Fennarith is a calculator. Equations, results, and the history you keep are worked out and stored on your phone. None of it is sent anywhere. The app has no account, and it does not ask for your name, contacts, photos, or location.

Fennarith does not run a server of its own and does not sell information.

Advertising

The free version of Fennarith shows a banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To choose an advertisement, AdMob receives your device's advertising ID along with technical information about the device, such as its model, screen size, operating system version, approximate region, and IP address. This is handled by Google, not by Fennarith, and is described in Google's own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your equations, results, and history are never part of this. The maths is worked out on the device and is not sent to AdMob or to anyone else.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Settings, Google, Ads.

Purchases

You can turn the banner off, either with a monthly subscription or with one payment. Purchases are handled by Google Play. Fennarith never sees or stores your card details. Google Play tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

A subscription can be cancelled at any time in the Google Play app, under Payments and subscriptions.

Children

Fennarith is intended for ages 13 and over.

Removing your data

Removing the app removes the history stored by the app. There is nothing stored elsewhere to delete, because nothing about your maths leaves the phone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
