Fennarith Algebra Solver Pro

An algebra desk for your phone

Fennarith calculates, solves, graphs, and writes the reason next to the answer. The maths runs on the device, so it keeps working without a connection.

3x + 7 = 22
Step 1 — subtract 7
3x = 15
Step 2 — divide by 3
x = 5

What it does

  • Linear and quadratic equations, with the steps
  • Higher polynomials by rational roots, then a numeric check
  • Two-equation systems
  • Derivatives and integrals
  • Graphing, with pinch, pan and trace
  • Decimal to fraction, including repeating decimals
  • Trigonometry, logarithms, roots, powers, factorial, degree and radian
