Privacy policy

Duskarith — webcam security video recorder and light adjuster

Effective November 27, 2026

Overview

Duskarith is a desktop webcam application for Windows. It records video, adjusts camera settings automatically based on the time of day, and detects motion in the video feed. Your privacy is important, so here is exactly what the program does and does not do with your data.

Data that stays on your computer

All recordings and settings stay on your computer until you enable FTP upload to your own specific FTP server that you configure yourself. Duskarith does not connect to the internet on its own unless the FTP upload is Enabled or unless the Web Stream Server is Enabled and Duskarith collects no personal data. All recordings are stored locally on your computer by default. The application does not automatically upload, transmit, or share your recordings with external services. Optional FTP upload and built-in web server features are available for users who want remote access or file transfers. These features are disabled by default and require explicit user configuration and activation. You remain in control of your recordings and how they are accessed or transferred.

No network communication

Duskarith does not connect to the internet unless the user enables FTP or the Web Stream Server. It does not send analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or usage statistics. It does not check for updates over the network. The only network-capable component is FFmpeg, which is used solely for local video encoding and playback.

No personal information collected

Duskarith does not collect, store, or process any personal information such as your name, email address, location, or device identifiers. The program has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no registration.

Camera access

Duskarith accesses your webcam through Windows DirectShow to display live video, record footage, and adjust camera controls such as brightness, contrast, and exposure. The camera feed is processed entirely on your computer and is never sent anywhere.

On-Device Neural Network

Duskarith's image correction and its object detection both run entirely on your computer, as local neural network models through Windows ML and ONNX (Microsoft's on-device machine-learning runtime). The light-adjustment models also retrain themselves on your own camera's frames, on your machine. No images, camera frames, models, or detection results are ever uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

Children's privacy

Duskarith does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. There are no user accounts, no online features, and no data collection of any kind.

Third-party services

Duskarith does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.
