Privacy policy

Duskarith — webcam security video recorder and light adjuster

Effective November 27, 2026

Overview

Duskarith is a desktop webcam application for Windows. It records video, adjusts camera settings automatically based on the time of day, and detects motion in the video feed. Your privacy is important, so here is exactly what the program does and does not do with your data.

Data that stays on your computer

All recordings and settings stay on your computer. Duskarith never uploads, transmits or shares your recordings, camera images or detection results, except snapshots sent to your own server if you turn on FTP/SFTP upload. The developer receives no data about you. You remain in control of your recordings and how they are accessed or transferred.

Network connections

Duskarith sends no analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or usage statistics. It makes only these connections:

  • Microsoft Store, to check your license and 30-day free trial, restore a purchase, and check for Duskarith updates. These checks are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Approximate location for sunrise and sunset. About once a week, Duskarith looks up the city-level location of your internet connection from ipinfo.io (or ip-api.com as a backup); these services see your IP address. The result is saved only on your computer. Duskarith then gets sunrise, sunset and time-zone information from api.sunrise-sunset.org, timeapi.io or open-meteo.com, sending only that approximate location.
  • Accurate time. Once a day Duskarith checks the clock against Microsoft's time server (time.windows.com), with public web servers as a fallback. Nothing about you is sent.
  • FTP/SFTP upload, only when you turn it on, and only to the server you set up yourself.

The Web Stream Server is available only on your own computer (localhost) and does not connect to the internet. FTP/SFTP upload and the Web Stream Server are off until you turn them on.

Personal information

Duskarith does not collect your name, email address, or device identifiers, and has no Duskarith account or sign-in. Your license is managed by Microsoft Store. The approximate location used for sunrise and sunset is kept only on your computer and is never sent to the developer.

Camera access

Duskarith accesses your webcam through Windows DirectShow to display live video, record footage, and adjust camera controls such as brightness, contrast, and exposure. The camera feed is processed entirely on your computer and is never sent anywhere.

On-Device Neural Network

Duskarith's image correction and its object detection both run entirely on your computer, as local neural network models through Windows ML and ONNX (Microsoft's on-device machine-learning runtime). The light-adjustment models also retrain themselves on your own camera's frames, on your machine. No images, camera frames, models, or detection results are ever uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

Children's privacy

The developer does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Duskarith has no user accounts and no advertising.

Third-party services

Duskarith has no advertising networks or analytics platforms. The only third-party services it contacts are the Microsoft Store, location, sunrise/sunset, time-zone and time services listed under Network connections above, and your own FTP/SFTP server if you set one up.
