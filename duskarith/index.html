Duskarith

Webcam security video recorder and light adjuster for Windows

What it does

Duskarith turns any USB webcam into an automatic security camera. It adjusts brightness, contrast, and exposure throughout the day — from sunrise through sunset into night — so the picture always looks right without touching a single slider.

Features

  • Automatic light adjustment with sunrise and sunset scheduling
  • Five time-of-day presets: bright sun, day, golden AM, golden PM, and night
  • Self-trained light brains that learn your camera's best settings, accelerated by Windows ML and ONNX — they run on your GPU or NPU through DirectML, with no TensorFlow and no CUDA to install
  • Each brain retrains itself on your own camera, on device
  • Smooth video that never drops below 15 frames per second, day or night
  • Continuous recording with configurable loop storage
  • Object detection for people and vehicles using NanoDet
  • Lock-on target tracking with automatic snapshots
  • Archive search by date and time, with clip export
  • 19 camera controls with real-time sliders
  • Night image enhancement with CLAHE and chroma denoising
  • Works with any DirectShow-compatible USB webcam
  • Resolutions up to 4K (3840 × 2160)

Privacy

All recordings and settings stay on your computer. Duskarith does not connect to the internet and collects no personal data. Read the full privacy policy.
