Fennarith Algebra Solver Pro for Windows

Privacy policy

This policy covers Fennarith Algebra Solver Pro for Windows, published by David Roark.

Your math stays on your PC

Fennarith processes the expressions, equations and other values you enter on your computer. The app keeps the current worksheet and its results in memory while it is open. Fennarith does not send your math inputs, graphs or results to CodeProSpecialist.

Accounts, ads and analytics

The app does not ask you to create a Fennarith account. The Windows app does not display ads or include analytics or tracking for math activity.

Free trial and purchase

Fennarith Algebra Solver Pro for Windows offers a 30-day free trial. A purchase is required to continue using the app after the trial ends. If you purchase through Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes your payment and manages your Store transaction. Fennarith does not ask for your payment card details. Microsoft Privacy Statement

Closing the app and managing Store data

Closing Fennarith discards the current worksheet. If you want to manage data in your Microsoft account, including information associated with your activity in Microsoft services, use Microsoft’s privacy controls. Microsoft privacy dashboard

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the publisher support details on the Fennarith Algebra Solver Pro for Windows Microsoft Store listing.
